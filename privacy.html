Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy describes how the Baseline Review Tool (“the tool”) handles information. The tool is a web application that runs in your browser.

What the tool processes

Schedule files (P6 XER files), contract documents and other files you choose to open. They are processed locally in your browser. The tool does not send them to the developer and the developer does not receive or store them.

Saved projects in your browser

If auto-save is on, project settings, results and (optionally) the files you opened are stored in your browser's own storage on your device. You can switch this off or clear it in the tool's settings, or by clearing the site data in your browser.

Google Drive (optional)

If you switch on Google Drive and sign in, the tool asks for access to Google Drive using the drive.file permission (or, only if you choose to use a folder you created yourself, the full Drive permission). The tool uses this access only to create and update folders and files for your projects (project files, reports and source files) in your Drive, and to open them again when you ask. It does not read other files in your Drive when the default permission is used.

  • The sign-in token is kept only in your browser's memory for the current session and is not sent to the developer.
  • Files saved to Drive belong to you and stay in your Google account. The developer has no access to them.
  • You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the files in your Drive.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Claude AI (optional, off by default)

If you switch AI on and give consent, selected content (for example activity names, day counts and lever descriptions, depending on the step) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to produce suggestions. The request goes either directly from your browser or through a small proxy on Cloudflare that adds the API key and does not store the content. Anthropic processes it under its own terms and privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you switch AI on and consent.

Hosting

The application is hosted on Cloudflare Workers behind a password. The host may keep standard technical logs (such as request times and IP addresses) as part of operating the service.

What the developer does not do

  • Does not sell or share your data.
  • Does not use your data for advertising.
  • Does not keep copies of your schedules, documents or Drive files.

Your choices

You can leave Google Drive and AI switched off, clear saved projects, disconnect Drive, and revoke permissions at any time.
