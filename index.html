Baseline Review Tool

A tool for Primavera P6 schedule review, built for planning engineers.

The Baseline Review Tool reviews P6 baseline and update schedules (XER files): schedule quality checks, comparison of an update with the baseline, delay analysis, recovery planning, baseline building and claim review. It is used by project-management consultants and planning engineers.

How it works

  • Schedule files are read and analysed in your own browser. They are not uploaded to a server.
  • Optional: you can save your work to your own Google Drive, in a folder you control.
  • Optional: you can switch on Claude AI for suggestions; this is off by default and needs your consent.

Open the tool

The application is access-controlled (password): https://baseline-review-tool.mohd2018me.workers.dev
